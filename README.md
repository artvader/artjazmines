# Art Jazmines — Portfolio

The personal portfolio website of Art Jazmines, a frontend and UI developer with a background in visual design. It brings together selected work, professional experience, and services, showing how design becomes purposeful, usable interfaces.

## What’s inside

- **About:** Art’s background and approach to frontend development, visual design, and digital products.
- **Selected work:** Projects including Music Museum Group, LAPR+, and Lutong Bahay, alongside website, brochure, and magazine design work.
- **Services:** Web design, print design, branding, and interactive prototyping.
- **Contact:** A way to get in touch about projects and collaborations.

## Visual direction

The site takes inspiration from a sci-fi foundry: dark surfaces, amber light, holographic imagery, and motion that responds to scrolling. Koulen display headings, Lato body text, and Lekton system labels give the portfolio its mix of bold typography and industrial detail.

## Built with

React, TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind CSS, with Swiper for project galleries and Formspree for contact submissions. Images, video, and locally hosted fonts support the site’s visual identity.
